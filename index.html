Popular roles now draw up to 50% more applications than in prior years, largely from candidates using AI to submit in bulk. More volume, not better matches, which is exactly why screeners tightened up.

<1s

Rejected before a human ever opens the file

A recruiter might spend minutes on a resume, but an AI layer can decline it in under a second if it isn't optimized. ATS is now just the first of three or four AI systems scoring you before anyone human weighs in.

LLMs

Workday, Greenhouse & Ashby now read like a person

Major platforms have shipped LLM screening that summarizes each resume in plain language, scores it against the role, and drafts recruiter notes. Old keyword-stuffing tricks no longer clear the bar. Meaning matters now.

Your edge: real, quantified accomplishments beat keyword stuffing when an LLM is reading.

Questions

Answered plainly.

How does the free CV review actually work?
Upload your resume (PDF or Word) along with the role you're targeting, your desired salary, and any notes. One of our resume strategists reviews it by hand against your target role and sends you a written report: strengths, issues, and exactly what to fix, within 24 hours.
Is my resume stored or shared?
Your file is used only to prepare your review and isn't sold or shared. It's emailed directly to our team to review.
What do I get back?
A clear, written read on what's already working in your favor, what's likely hurting your chances with both recruiters and AI screeners, and the specific changes to make, written in plain language by a person, not an automated score.
What's included in a resume rewrite?
A full rebuild engineered to parse cleanly, mirror the language of your target roles, and lead with quantified outcomes, written by hand, not auto-generated. Turnaround is typically a couple of days, with revisions to align on direction.
Do you work with senior and executive resumes?
Yes, much of our work is with experienced professionals leveling up. Senior resumes often score lower than expected because scope and impact get buried; the fixes are usually small but decisive.

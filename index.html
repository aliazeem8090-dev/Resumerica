    Questions

    Answered plainly.

    How does the free scan actually work?
    You upload a PDF and it's analyzed the way a modern applicant-tracking system would read it — a blend of classic keyword parsing and the LLM screening now shipping in platforms like Workday, Greenhouse and Ashby. You get a 0–100 score, a category breakdown, your strengths, the specific fixes to make, and the keywords you're missing.
    Is my resume stored or shared?
    No. Your file is analyzed in-session to produce your report and isn't retained. In production, report delivery connects to your own email provider — nothing is sold or shared.
    What score do I actually need?
    As a rule of thumb, 70+ tends to clear the first automated gate, and 85+ reads as a strong candidate. Below 70, most screeners filter you out before a recruiter looks — which is where a rewrite pays for itself.
    What's included in a resume rewrite?
    A full rebuild engineered to parse cleanly, mirror the language of your target roles, and lead with quantified outcomes — written by hand, not auto-generated. Turnaround is typically a couple of days, with revisions to align on direction.
    Do you work with senior and executive resumes?
    Yes — much of our work is with experienced professionals leveling up. Senior resumes often score lower than expected because scope and impact get buried; the fixes are usually small but decisive.
